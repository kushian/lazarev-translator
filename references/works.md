# Lazarev's works — Russian ↔ Arabic titles

Use Sablabal's Arabic title when one exists (✅ = seen in his translations or on his publisher's
pages). Titles marked (مقترح) have no known Sablabal translation: propose one in his style,
tell the user it is a proposal, and record it with `status.py title`.

Sablabal's translations are distributed as digital editions on lazarev.ru and in print by
Dar Iqraani (دار إقرأني). Another translator (منتجب يونس، دار علاء الدين، دمشق 2010) used other
titles («تشخيص الكارما»، «الكارما النقية»); prefer Sablabal's.
